# Airport-zone pickup capacity under asymmetric scenario costs

## Decision, target and limits

Compare a median with higher empirical conditional quantiles for an explicitly **retrospective, idealized** one-service-per-vehicle-hour allocation simulation. The target is the published count of yellow-taxi records with meter engagement in TLC zones132 (JFK Airport) or138 (LaGuardia Airport), during2024. It is neither latent passenger demand nor rider/driver wait time. Use the official historical NYC Open Data dataset rwwi-khbc and official zone lookup. Fix the protocol before querying any trip-count outcomes. Only metadata, schema, zone labels and official documentation have been inspected.

The historical dataset was published in2026; TLC generally releases monthly files with a delay. Exact historical source vintages and dispatcher telemetry are unavailable. The simulation assumes complete prior-day counts are immediately accessible and uses the captured historical record version. Therefore it cannot validate prospective forecast availability, actual dispatch, queue waits or savings. Those are explicit unknowns outside this finite conditional comparison. No live deployment or genuine real-time leakage clearance is claimed.

No trip IDs, vehicle/driver IDs, individual routes, dropoffs, fares, passenger fields or exact trip timestamps are requested. Preserve every query, response hash and metadata revision. Require matching source row-update/version metadata before and after and exact independent daily/monthly reconciliation. Reject duplicate airport/day/hour groups, negative/noninteger counts, out-of-scope groups and truncation. Fill an absent server group with zero **published records**, not a claim of no true demand or complete reporting.

Construct all nominal civil date/hour bins for both zones in2024. Since timestamps are floating local labels and repeated/skipped DST hours are ambiguous, exclude the entire2024-03-10 and2024-11-03 dates from both model history and targets, while retaining and reporting their source counts. Do not convert these bins into elapsed UTC hours. Do not remove low counts, outliers or holidays. Non-airport, null-zone and invalid-date source records are outside the target and must be quantified when the source query supports them. No completeness or deduplication ground truth is available.

## Fixed candidates, validation and selection

January-June supplies initial history; July, August and September are three forward validation months. Held targets are October-December. At each nominal target date, use only prior dates in [date-W,date), excluding the two fixed DST dates. History is zone-specific and the same civil hour. Weekday/hour groups match the exact weekday; daytype/hour groups match weekday-versus-weekend. W is28,56 or84days. Empirical quantiles are1/2,3/4,9/10, using the observed order statistic at ceiling(q*n), with n>=3. No interpolation or learned regression model. Retain all18 calendar/window/quantile candidates and all observation-level validation predictions; every history boundary/count and candidate score must be inspectable.

The baseline is the most recent available same-weekday/hour observed count within28prior days, excluding DST dates. Any unavailable prediction is retained with its reason; primary comparisons require complete common target coverage rather than silently dropping hard cases. The fixed design expects4,416 validation and4,368 held zone-hour targets, with8,688 initial-history bins, but verify actual calendar construction independently.

For demand d and proposed count p, hypothetical capacity a=min(p,K). Unmet=max(d-a,0), excess=max(a-d,0); cost=cu*unmet+excess. K={100,250,500} services per zone/hour; cu={1,3,9}; overage cost1. These are untuned hypothetical units, not currency or measured vehicle supply. Primary K=250,cu=3. Select a calendar/window for each quantile by total pooled validation primary cost; then select the primary winner from those three. Exact ties prefer daytype/hour, then shorter window, then smaller quantile. This is finite grid selection, not a globally optimal forecasting method. Retain all18 candidate statuses, per-month/airport metrics and runtimes. Freeze the source, code and selections before held analytical outcomes.

Evaluate the three frozen quantile policies and baseline on all held targets, all nine cap/cost settings and all airport/month subgroups. Prior held counts can enter later held histories under the explicitly idealized sequential-availability assumption; no future target date enters any history. Test the fixed November28 and December25 stress dates separately and together; these are OPM2024 holiday dates, not an exhaustive holiday effect or causal comparison.
